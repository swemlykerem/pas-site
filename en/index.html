Security

Your data stays with you.

You decide who sees what and which data leaves your company; every change is on record.

On your computer

Runs on your own computer or server; your business data stays there.

Passkey sign-in

Password and passkey are supported; administrator actions are re-verified with a passkey.

Audit log

Important actions are written to a tamper-evident audit log.

Masking

Personal data sent to AI is masked; a cross-border transfer record is kept automatically.

Per-department access

Each employee sees only their department's screens and data.

Approved changes

Actions that save data go through the approval rules you define.

Tech specs.

Data sources
.xlsx and legacy .xls; CSV (UTF-8 or Windows-1254, comma- or semicolon-separated, decimal comma); Turkish e-invoice and e-archive XML.
Import
Preview before loading; row-by-row accounting (imported, skipped, left out and why); amount reconciliation; undo the last load; Excel report of rows left out.
Apps
Sales order, purchase request, expenses, leave request, stock count, production and scrap, maintenance and faults, quality control; dashboard, screen and app drafts from your description.
AI
Claude (Anthropic) with your own key; monthly spending limit; answers with sources; one model drafts, a second model reviews and you approve. PAS also works without a key.
Access
In the browser, from any computer on your company network.
Sign-in and access
Password and passkey; passkey re-verification for administrator actions; per-department access.
Logging and privacy
Tamper-evident audit log; personal data sent to AI is masked; a cross-border transfer record is kept automatically.
Installation and language
Your company's own Windows computer or server; Turkish and English interface.
