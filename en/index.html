Security

Your data stays with you.

PAS is installed on your company's own computer or server. You decide who sees and changes what.

On your computer

Runs on your own computer or server; business data is kept there.

Passkey sign-in

Password and passkey; administrator actions require passkey re-verification.

Audit log

Important actions are written to a tamper-evident audit log.

Masking

Personal data sent to AI is masked; a cross-border transfer record is kept automatically.

Per-department access

Each employee sees only their department's screens and data.

Approved writes

Actions that save data pass the approval rule you define.
